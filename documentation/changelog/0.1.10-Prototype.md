## CHG-0503 · 2026-11-18 · The sidebar's creation rows rest on the conversation's own fill

Type: Code
Event: Delivery
Scope: Sidebar (creation rows)

Summary: The owner sent a screenshot of **Start a quest** under **Borderlands** and asked for its background to be white, the same as the conversation's. The row had no fill at rest (CHG-0481), so it read in the sidebar's own tone (`colors.sidebar`, `#ebebec` in One light) inside its thin border, which the owner read as a gray box. `buttonRest` in `apps/app/src/shell/railStyles.ts` now paints `colors.page`, the fill the conversation uses (`#fafafa` in One light, `#f3ece3` in Iskios light, `#282c33` in One dark, `#172230` in Iskios dark). The other creation row, **Visit a realm** under **Realms**, takes the same fill: the two were built as twins and one white row beside one gray row would read as a mistake. Everything else is unchanged: the thin rounded border, the size, the right-hand icon, the label, and the soft fill on hover (`colors.controlHover`). The two sections' labels are untouched.
Reason: The owner: "Try to make the background white for the button. Same as the background of chat."
References: CHG-0474 (the border), CHG-0481 (no fill at rest, which this replaces), CHG-0478 (a softer resting fill, dropped), [simplified left navigation](../capabilities/mission-tracking/specifications.md#simplified-left-navigation), `apps/app/src/shell/railStyles.ts`.
Validation: Biome clean and `tsc --noEmit` in `apps/app` clean; the app's tests pass (74 files, 534 passed, 6 skipped). Not seen in a window: the rows' look in light and dark and a hover, since the Electron window cannot open inside this thread's sandbox. The owner's look remains the check, as it was for the rows' earlier fills.
