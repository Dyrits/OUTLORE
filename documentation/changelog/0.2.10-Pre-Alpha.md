## CHG-0757 · 2026-11-26 · A sent image is not read back from the service after its own upload

Type: Code
Event: Agreement and delivery
Scope: Message attachments

Summary: The app already kept the images it fetched by attachment identifier (`imageCache`, 64 MB, least recently shown dropped first, one request for two simultaneous views), so a refresh, a scroll or another panel's change never fetched a sent image again.
The one read left was the first: the image the owner attached was sent to the service on upload, and its message then fetched the same bytes back with `ReadAttachment`.
The upload now puts the picture in the cache under the card's identifier (`imageCache.put`), so the sent message shows it with no request; removing or discarding a draft forgets it (`imageCache.forget`).
The cache is `sentImages` in `sentImage.ts`, shared by the message box and the thumbnails, and `dataAddressOf` writes the address both use.
Nothing changes on the service or the wire: `ReadAttachment` still serves another window, a restart, or an image from an older message.
Reason: The review's wave 2 asked that an image be read and sent once per screen (STD-PKG-02, 2026-11-26).
References: [message attachments](../capabilities/message-attachments/specifications.md), CHG-0750.
Validation: `imageCache.test.ts` (five new tests: put, no overwrite, eviction by a put, forget, a forgotten fetch that settles later) and `sentImage.test.ts` (two new), the first written red.
Not checked in a running desktop window.

## CHG-0758 · 2026-11-26 · A change tells a screen only the reads it concerns, and equal reads share one request

Type: Code
Event: Agreement and delivery
Scope: Host service change feed, app service client

Summary: A `Changed` frame now names the topics its changes touched, `work` and `spend`, when they touched only those (`topics`, absent when a change may have touched anything).
A turn's events (replies, activity, notices, steered messages, folder moves, admission and failure) announce `work`, and a turn's end announces `spend` as well; every other change stays unscoped.
`queryTopics` in `packages/contracts/src/topics.ts` lists the topics each read follows, and the app's `ServiceClient.subscribe` takes the tag of the read it refetches, so the settings, the Vault, the connectors, the paired hosts, the harnesses and the host's name no longer refetch while agents work.
A burst keeps one frame at once and one after the window, the second naming the union of what the burst touched, or none when one change in it was unscoped.
`ServiceClient.request` also gives one answer to equal reads asked together, so `ReadChronicle` asked from three places (and `ReadAccountBudgets` from four) is one frame on the wire, not three; the sharing ends at the next `Changed` frame and at any command, so a read asked after a change never takes an answer that may predate it.
Reason: The idle host traffic record recommended sharing identical queries and then scoping `Changed` (CHG-0724), and the review's second wave listed scoped topics (STD-PKG-01 part 2).
Each `Changed` made every mounted call site refetch, `ReadSettings` from up to 12 sites and `ReadChronicle` at 18 KB with 30 quests, about 120 KB with all mounted.
Safe by default: a read not named in `queryTopics` and a change that names no topics both refetch as before, and so does a screen that reconnects.
Not scoped yet, and open: the other publishers (lore, settings and connector changes, thread tools) stay unscoped, and the measurement of the loopback link and the relay's request count was not repeated, so the saving is shown by the tests below and not yet by a live reading.
References: [Architecture](../architecture.md), [verification of idle host traffic](../verification/2026-11-25-idle-host-traffic.md), CHG-0719, CHG-0724.
Validation: `packages/contracts/test/topics.test.ts` (the table names real requests and every request that reads by name), `useServiceQuery.test.ts` (a scoped change refetches only the reads that follow its topics; equal reads share one request until a change or a command), `idleTraffic.test.ts` (a turn's frames all name topics and the end names `spend`; unscoped and scoped bursts) and `commands.test.ts` passed, and the typecheck passes in all seven workspaces. Not checked in a running desktop window.

## CHG-0759 · 2026-11-26 · Booleans take a prefix across packages, a few casts go, and the quest title menu stops redrawing

Type: Code
Event: Delivery
Scope: Relay client and route, controller, service, app; conventions sweep (review wave 2)

Summary: The booleans the earlier sweep left across package boundaries now carry a prefix.
`usesLegacyClaim` is `isUsingLegacyClaim`, `legacyFallback` is `shouldFallBackToLegacyClaim`, `allowLegacyClaim` is `shouldAllowLegacyClaim`, `acceptOtherRelease` is `shouldAcceptOtherRelease` and `allowLocalRelay` is `shouldAllowLocalRelay`, renamed together with their callers in `packages/relay`, `packages/controller`, `packages/service` and `apps/app`; the relay Worker bundle was rebuilt (`bun run bundle`), so its source hash changed.
Booleans local to one package were renamed too: `needsRefresh`, `takesTurns` (`canTakeTurns`), `includeFullContext`, `allowsMetered`, `hideExitBar`, `showErrands`, `showQuests`, `initiallyShowErrands`, `usesCodeMode`, `keepsPath`, `signInNeedsNoTerminal` (`canSignInWithoutTerminal`), `advertisesHttp`, `preserveProviderErrors`, `includeArchived`, `writesFiles` (`canWriteFiles`), `needsPairing`, `wasUpdated`, and the app's `busy` and the pairing block's `copied` prop.
Names fixed by a wire or stored format (`includeRituals` in the contracts, `acceptsFeedback` in the Muse protocol, Electron's updater fields, React Native and WebRTC options) keep their names, and so do the ones left for a later sweep (`granted`, `closed`, `exited`).
Casts that were not brands or generic seams were removed: error messages come through the new `errorMessageOf` (core) and `messageOf` (app) instead of `(error as Error)`; `railActions.tsx` and `questDrag.web.tsx` narrow the DOM value with `instanceof`; the Ritual rows in `questListRows.tsx` narrow with `flatMap` instead of `row.thread?.id as ThreadId`, which would have handed `undefined` on; `keyFile.ts` and `ownCopies.ts` read `code` and `stderr` after an `in` check; three `as unknown` and empty-array casts went.
The casts left are brands (`as MissionId` and its kind), `Object.keys(...) as ReadonlyArray<keyof Settings>`, `sidebarOverlay`'s web-only style, and the generic result casts in `service.ts`, `useServiceQuery.ts`, `client.ts` and the two `Results[tag] as unknown as Schema.Codec<unknown>` decoders; each stands for a type TypeScript cannot follow.
The `QuestTitle` audit found that it subscribes to the shared missions query, so it renders whenever any quest in any realm changes, and rebuilt its `current` object and `choices` list on every render, so the whole menu (chip icon, `Menu`) drew again each time.
`QuestTitle` now maps the realm's quests to `{ id, state, title }`, keeps the previous list while `sameTitleChoices` says the menu would look the same, and hands it to a memoized `QuestTitleMenu`, so a change to another realm's quest, or to a quest's other fields (last activity, counts), no longer redraws the menu.
The errand title is unchanged: it renders from its own thread's detail.
Reason: Review wave 2 (owner, 2026-11-26): the names the sweeps could not change without crossing packages, the casts they did not reach, and the audit of `QuestTitle` re-renders.
References: [conventions](../conventions.md), CHG-0752.
Validation: Biome reported no error; the 7-workspace typecheck passed; `apps/app/test` conversation, foundation, shell and rituals folders and `packages/relay` and `packages/controller` passed (787 tests, the Worker bundle hash test after a rebuild); `packages/service/test` remote, update, harness, vault and core passed 697 tests with 54 skipped (two `idleTraffic.test.ts` tests failed in the parallel run, as in the handoff of 2026-11-26-0725, and passed alone in 6.7 s); `packages/service/test` work, policy, lore, connectors and fixtures passed 1228 tests (one memory-limit test of `codeExecutor.test.ts` failed in the parallel run and passed alone in 5 s); the new `titleChoices.test.ts` passes. Not measured: the render counts of `QuestTitle` in the running app.

## CHG-0761 · 2026-11-26 · The phone gets a floating pill of three tabs and a Menu button

Type: Code
Event: Agreement and delivery
Scope: Phone shell (narrow window)

Summary: On a narrow window a translucent pill floats over the content with three tabs, Chronicle, Quests and Chronicler, each a full screen of its own; the lit tab shows its name, and the Chronicle tab carries the Attention count.
The Quests tab is the realm and quest tree of the old full-screen menu, without its Chronicle row and Workshop foot.
The Chronicler tab is the existing Chronicler chat, with its composer kept clear above the pill.
The top bar names the tab and has, furthest right on every screen, a Menu button (a grid of four squares) that opens a screen of tiles for Lore, Archive, Reserves and Settings; a place opened from it is named in the top bar, which offers Back to the Menu.
In a quest or errand the pill gives way to the conversation's own composer; the quest panel and the Ritual form show no pill.
The pill shrinks to a circle at the left while a screen scrolls down and returns on a scroll up, near the top, or a press on the circle.
The phone starts on the Chronicle tab.
Back from a quest returns to the Chronicle, the channel, Chronicler, or the Quests tab it was opened from; `QuestOrigin` gains `chronicler`, and `Location.isMenuOpen` (the old full-screen menu) is now `isQuestsOpen`, with a new `isMenuOpen` for the Menu of places.
Wide windows are unchanged.
Defaults taken: a Ritual is not a place (CHG-0616), so it stays in its realm's rows on the Quests tab and the Menu has no Rituals tile; Realms are on the Quests tab, so the Menu has no Realms tile; the pill stays on the Chronicler tab, with the chat's composer above it; there is no blur outside browsers, and the pill's width change is the browser's own transition, because the app does not use `Animated` or reanimated; the pill's bottom gap is a fixed 24 on a phone, because the app has no safe-area package; the conversation keeps its Back chevron in its header rather than a Back button in the composer.
Reason: The owner chose direction E of the phone shell page for the phone, with three tabs and the Menu in the top bar (2026-11-26, CHG-0760).
References: [mobile shell page](../art-direction/2026-11-26-mobile-shell.html), [Conversation panes specification](../capabilities/conversation-panes/specifications.md#phone-channels), CHG-0646, CHG-0647.
Validation: `phoneShell.test.ts` (new) and `location.test.ts` pass with the rest of `apps/app/test/shell`, and the workspace typecheck passes.
The web export ran against a real service on isolated data (fake harness, three realms and quests) in headless Chrome at 390 by 844 with the owner's Look: the three tabs, the Menu and its four places with Back, a quest with no pill, Back to the Quests tab, the Attention count falling as a quest was read, and the pill measured at 366, 56 and 366 points across a scroll down and up, in One dark and light, Iskios dark, Mono light and Slate dark; a 1280-wide window was unchanged.
Not checked: a real phone, Android gesture or three-button navigation under the pill, the pill's blur on a phone, Safari, and a long Chronicler thread with the keyboard open.
