# 0.2.10-Pre-Alpha

## CHG-0756 · 2026-11-26 · The History tab can show archived quests

Type: Code
Event: Agreement and delivery
Scope: Realm channel, History tab

Summary: The History tab has a "Show archived quests" switch above the list, off by default and kept only while the tab is open.
Switched on, the realm's (or the Borderlands') archived quests join the list under their last-activity headings, each line reading "Archived" before its harness and model and offering no archive button; pressing one opens it as the Archived view does.
`historyQuestsOf` picks the quests of the tab, and `archivedMissions` is the shared query for the Archived view's missions.
Reason: The owner asked that the History tab in the realm allow showing or hiding archived quests (2026-11-26).
The switch is local to the tab rather than an owner setting; a setting would be a small follow-up if wanted.
References: [Mission tracking specification](../capabilities/mission-tracking/specifications.md), CHG-0686.
Validation: `CI=1 bun run test apps/app/test/shell/questTree.test.ts` passed 26 tests (two new for `historyQuestsOf`) and Biome reports no error, and the app typecheck passes. Not checked in a running desktop window.

## CHG-0757 · 2026-11-26 · Ten phone shell directions drawn for the owner to choose from

Type: Documentation
Event: Agreement
Scope: Phone shell, art direction

Summary: A self-contained page of ten live phone mockups (A to J) compares different information architectures for the phone's bottom bar: a Slack-style four tabs (A), a Duet-style bar with the Chronicler in the middle (B), a raised "new quest" button (C), Chronicle / Realms / Quests / More (D), a floating pill that collapses on scroll (E), a "now working" strip above the bar (F), realms as tabs (G), two tabs and one composer with a drawer (H), an inbox segmented by Attention, Running and Done (I), and a thin bar with a sheet that expands (J).
Each shows the Chronicle with its Attention badge, a quest conversation with the composer pinned, and the footer places (Lore, Archive, Reserves, Settings); a comparison table and a recommendation (D first, then B) follow the grid.
No app code changed.
Reason: The owner asked that the phone look more like Claude Duet or the Slack mobile app, with a bottom bar like theirs (2026-11-26), and wanted options before any code changes.
References: [mobile shell page](../art-direction/2026-11-26-mobile-shell.html), [Claude Duet research](../research/claude-duet-2026-11-22.md), CHG-0647 (the phone Chronicle batch).
Validation: The page opened in headless Chrome for Testing in dark and light with no script error and no element past a phone's edge; every phone's bar, a quest, Back, the sheets and drawers, the answer-an-Attention-card count, the floating pill's collapse and the sheet's detents were driven and screenshotted. Not checked on a real phone or in Safari.

## CHG-0760 · 2026-11-26 · The phone gets a floating pill of three tabs and a Menu button

Type: Code
Event: Agreement and delivery
Scope: Phone shell (narrow window)

Summary: On a narrow window a translucent pill floats over the content with three tabs, Chronicle, Quests and Chronicler, each a full screen of its own; the lit tab shows its name, and the Chronicle tab carries the Attention count.
The Quests tab is the realm and quest tree of the old full-screen menu, without its Chronicle row and Workshop foot.
The Chronicler tab is the existing Chronicler chat, with its composer kept clear above the pill.
The top bar names the tab and has, furthest right on every screen, a Menu button (a grid of four squares) that opens a screen of tiles for Lore, Archive, Reserves and Settings; a place opened from it is named in the top bar, which offers Back to the Menu.
In a quest or errand the pill gives way to the conversation's own composer; the quest panel and the Ritual form show no pill.
The pill shrinks to a circle at the left while a screen scrolls down and returns on a scroll up, near the top, or a press on the circle.
The phone starts on the Chronicle tab.
Back from a quest returns to the Chronicle, the channel, Chronicler, or the Quests tab it was opened from; `QuestOrigin` gains `chronicler`, and `Location.isMenuOpen` (the old full-screen menu) is now `isQuestsOpen`, with a new `isMenuOpen` for the Menu of places.
Wide windows are unchanged.
Defaults taken: a Ritual is not a place (CHG-0616), so it stays in its realm's rows on the Quests tab and the Menu has no Rituals tile; Realms are on the Quests tab, so the Menu has no Realms tile; the pill stays on the Chronicler tab, with the chat's composer above it; there is no blur outside browsers, and the pill's width change is the browser's own transition, because the app does not use `Animated` or reanimated; the pill's bottom gap is a fixed 24 on a phone, because the app has no safe-area package; the conversation keeps its Back chevron in its header rather than a Back button in the composer.
Reason: The owner chose direction E of the phone shell page for the phone, with three tabs and the Menu in the top bar (2026-11-26, CHG-0757).
References: [mobile shell page](../art-direction/2026-11-26-mobile-shell.html), [Conversation panes specification](../capabilities/conversation-panes/specifications.md#phone-channels), CHG-0646, CHG-0647.
Validation: `phoneShell.test.ts` (new) and `location.test.ts` pass with the rest of `apps/app/test/shell`, and the workspace typecheck passes.
The web export ran against a real service on isolated data (fake harness, three realms and quests) in headless Chrome at 390 by 844 with the owner's Look: the three tabs, the Menu and its four places with Back, a quest with no pill, Back to the Quests tab, the Attention count falling as a quest was read, and the pill measured at 366, 56 and 366 points across a scroll down and up, in One dark and light, Iskios dark, Mono light and Slate dark; a 1280-wide window was unchanged.
Not checked: a real phone, Android gesture or three-button navigation under the pill, the pill's blur on a phone, Safari, and a long Chronicler thread with the keyboard open.
