## CHG-0782 · 2026-11-27 · The phone's Quests tab, hold, sheets and top bar follow the owner's remarks

Type: Code
Event: Delivery
Scope: Phone bar and sheets, `apps/app/src/shell`, `apps/app/src/conversation/threadPaneHeader.tsx`
Summary: The Quests tab always draws crossed swords, with a gold dot at their corner once a quest is chosen (it drew one sword before). A tab opens its sheet after a hold of a quarter of a second (half a second before). Each sheet starts with a creation row: **New realm** (the sidebar's naming dialog), **New quest** (an empty quest in the chosen realm, through `useStartQuest`, opened on success) and **New errand** (the quest's errand starter). On a quest the phone's top bar runs the desktop pane header's order: Mark as done, Archive, New quest, New errand, then the sword; New errand used to come first and New quest was missing.
Reason: The owner asked for the logo, a shorter hold, creation in every menu, and top icons that match the desktop.
References: [conversation-panes specifications](../capabilities/conversation-panes/specifications.md) (phone bar, tab and sheet bullets), [verification](../verification/2026-11-27-phone-quest-tab-and-sheets.md), CHG-0773.
Validation: Biome, the app typecheck and the app tests pass (128 files, 1,048 passed, 6 skipped); `creationOf` and the sheet's row are covered by tests shown able to fail. Not run: a phone or a window, so the dot, the hold, what pressing a row does and the top bar's order are unproven; the owner's check on a phone is the acceptance.
Evidence: the verification record above.

## CHG-0786 · 2026-11-27 · Mentioned Companion answers sit in a frame under their message

Type: Code
Event: Delivery
Scope: Thread messaging, `packages/contracts/src/threadRecords.ts`, `packages/service/src/work/missionDetail.ts`, `apps/app/src/conversation`, `apps/app/src/foundation/messageStyles.ts`
Summary: `Message` gains an optional `isCompanionAnswer` flag set by the host from `companion_exchanges.reply_message_id` (older paired hosts and clients remain compatible).
`entriesOf` attaches each Companion answer to its tagged message in chronological order instead of leaving it at the bottom of the feed, carrying its anchored reply lines with it; when the tagged message is not drawn (such as a pinned root), the answer stands alone right after that message's time with Companion tokens stripped from its "Replying to" quote.
`EntryView` draws the tagged message and its answers inside one bordered, softly rounded `exchangeFrame` without the redundant "Replying to" line on answers.
`matchingMessages` and `targetIndexOf` find answers inside a frame at their carrying entry.
Reason: A tagged Companion's reply previously landed at the bottom of the feed with a truncated raw `@companion:...` quote, separated from the question whenever other messages arrived in between.
References: [thread-messaging specifications](../capabilities/thread-messaging/specifications.md#mentioning-a-companion-with-), [thread-messaging blueprint](../capabilities/thread-messaging/blueprint.md#mentioning-a-companion-with-), [verification](../verification/2026-11-27-companion-answer-frame.md), CHG-0781.
Validation: Biome on touched files, workspace typecheck, and 23 focused test files (150 tests: `apps/app/test/conversation` and `packages/service/test/work/companions.test.ts`) pass.
Not run: a real desktop window or phone in light and dark themes, so the frame's visual weight and spacing await human review.
Evidence: the verification record above.
