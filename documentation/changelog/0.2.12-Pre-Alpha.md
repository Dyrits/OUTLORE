## CHG-0782 · 2026-11-27 · The phone's Quests tab, hold, sheets and top bar follow the owner's remarks

Event: Delivery
Scope: Phone bar and sheets, `apps/app/src/shell`, `apps/app/src/conversation/threadPaneHeader.tsx`
Summary: The Quests tab always draws crossed swords, with a gold dot at their corner once a quest is chosen (it drew one sword before). Not run: a phone or a window, so the dot, the hold, what pressing a row does and the top bar's order are unproven; the owner's check on a phone is the acceptance.
Evidence: the verification record above.

## CHG-0789 · 2026-11-27 · The Companions tab splits its presets from the details, holds an unsaved Custom entry, and edits a preset in place

Type: Code
Event: Delivery
Scope: `apps/app/src/editors/companionPicker.tsx`, `apps/app/src/editors/presetChooser.tsx`, `apps/app/src/editors/presetEntries.ts`, `apps/app/src/editors/presetEditor.tsx`, `apps/app/src/editors/presets.ts`, `apps/app/src/editors/useThreadPresets.ts`, `documentation/capabilities/companion-presets/specifications.md`, `documentation/capabilities/thread-panel/specifications.md`, `documentation/glossary.md`
Summary: The Companions tab of a quest or errand measures its own width (`onLayout`, `panelLayoutOf` in `presetEntries.ts`): at `splitLeastWidth` (480) and above the presets stand in a left list (one row per preset: name over model · effort · account · N backups, `navWidth` 176) with the thread's Companion details (the Companion fold, the backup folds, Overwrite, Save as a preset) to their right, and narrower (the phone, the 288 mini panel, the 360 column) the sections stack as before, so a resized panel switches cleanly. When the thread's whole stack matches no preset and its backups are read, a **Custom** entry subtitled "not saved yet" follows the presets and takes the selection (`entriesOf`); the preset it came from keeps its row unchecked, pressing Custom does nothing, Save as a preset and Overwrite persist it, and it disappears once a preset matches; it is not shown while the backups are unread or when there are no presets. The details of a selected preset gain an **Edit** button at the top right that enters edit mode (`presetEditor.tsx`): the title row reads "Editing <name>", the same spot holds **Done**, and the details are replaced by the preset's own Companion and backup editors (the same `RunnerPickers`, `BackupsEditor` and `useOwnBackups` as the Workshop's panel), each change saved to the preset at once through the same `ChangeCompanionPreset` `SetStack` request (`presetStackRequest`), never `ChangeThreadRunner`, with a failure on the error line. A thread that was on the edited preset now matches no preset, so its selection moves to Custom holding its old stack and it rejoins the preset by pressing its row, an ordinary handover; a preset renamed or deleted meanwhile ends the edit mode. Rename and delete stay in the Workshop.
Reason: The owner asked (2026-11-27, settled with "both") for the presets to become a left navigation beside the details on a wide column, for the silent detach after changing a chosen preset to become a visible unsaved Custom entry, and for an explicit per-preset edit mode that persists to the preset while leaving every thread that used it as it is.
References: [companion presets specifications](../capabilities/companion-presets/specifications.md) (list layout, Custom entry, edit mode), [thread-panel specifications](../capabilities/thread-panel/specifications.md) (the Companions tab), [glossary](../glossary.md) (Custom entry, preset edit mode), CHG-0661.
Validation: Biome on the changed files (clean), the 7-workspace typecheck (exit 0), and `CI=1 bun run test apps/app` (131 files, 1,074 passed, 6 skipped by the sandbox, 0 failed); the three new test files (`presetEntries.test.ts`, `threadPresets.test.ts`, the additions to `companionPicker.test.ts`) fail on the starting commit and pass on the end state. Not run: the desktop window and a phone, so the split at 480, the look of Custom, Edit and Done, and a live edit are unproven.
