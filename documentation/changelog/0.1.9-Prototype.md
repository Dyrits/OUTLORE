# Changelog: 0.1.9-Prototype

## CHG-0500 · 2026-11-18 · Plan-limit rings keep the longer window outside

Type: Code
Event: Delivery
Scope: Sidebar (plan-limit rings), budget contract, service plan limits

Summary: The owner reported that the plan-limit rings look reversed. Cause: the rings were ordered by each window's reset time. A window whose reset has passed keeps its old reset time, so it sorted inside a window still counting down: with the weekly window reset and the 5-hour one at its limit, the 5-hour ring was drawn outside the weekly one, against the spec's "the weekly ring surrounds the 5-hour one". Fix: a plan-limit window now carries its length in minutes (`windowMinutes`, optional in the contract so readings saved before it decode), the service keeps it, and the rings are ordered by length, the longest outermost. A reading saved without a length keeps the old reset-time order until its next reading.
Reason: The hover card and the rings must agree, and the spec says the longer window surrounds the shorter.
References: CHG-0467, `apps/app/src/editors/planRings.ts` (`ringStackOf`), `apps/app/test/editors/planRings.test.ts`, `packages/contracts/src/budgets.ts` (`PlanLimitWindow`), `packages/service/src/work/planLimits.ts`, `packages/service/test/harness/codex.test.ts`, `packages/service/test/harness/openCodeTurns.test.ts`.
Validation: The new test in `planRings.test.ts`, built from the owner's screenshot state, failed before the fix (`['5-hour', 'Weekly']` instead of `['Weekly', '5-hour']`) and passes after it. The app suite passed in full (72 files, 499 tests, 6 skipped), typecheck passed on all seven workspaces, and Biome is clean on the changed files. The service's plan-limit and harness tests pass. The full service suite failed one lore fading test per run in two runs (different tests each time), and the lore tests pass alone (102 of 102); not caused by this change, not investigated further. Not seen in a window: the rings' drawing is inferred from the readout, since the screenshots were too small to read by eye.
