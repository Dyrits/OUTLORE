## CHG-0558 · 2026-11-19 · Conversation panes, batch 2: a quest and its errands open as a chain of panes

Type: Code
Event: Delivery
Scope: `apps/app` (shell, conversation, foundation), conversation panes batch 2

Summary: On a wide window a quest opens as panes side by side. On a window narrower than 820 px, and on a phone, one conversation shows at a time as before.
- **The chain is the path in the quest's tree** to the open thread: the quest's own conversation, then an errand beside it, then that errand's errand. Pressing an errand's **reply line** in a pane opens it in the next pane and drops every pane to the right of that one; choosing an errand in the sidebar builds its whole chain (a nested errand opens four panes, one per level); closing a pane (✕) drops it with what hangs from it, and the reply line in its parent opens it again. Because the chain is derived from the open thread, `location.ts` and `navigation.ts` did not change: `openThread` already is the deepest pane. (The blueprint said a chain would replace `openThread`; it was not needed.)
- **Up to four panes**, each at least 380 px, sharing the width equally (a quarter each with four). A longer chain shows the last four and folds the earlier ones to **strips** (44 px, the state's dot and the title running down); a window too narrow for the panes folds the earliest ones the same way. The deepest pane is always open. A strip is one press from open: it takes the room it needs and the earliest other panes fold in its place.
- **Fold** (‹) turns a pane into a strip, and the others share its room equally; the deepest pane cannot be folded. **Resize**: dragging the edge between two open panes moves room from one to the other, neither below 380 px; the shares are remembered (`outlore:pane-weights`), by depth in the chain.
- **Reply line** (was a text link "Opened an errand: …"): a card under the message the errand hangs from, with who opened it, its title, its state, how many messages wait, and how long ago it last moved; marked "Open" while its pane is open. It is the existing `ThreadOpened` entry, not yet the agent-opened system card of batch 4.
- **The details panel stays a right-hand column and follows the pane pressed last.** Each pane's header has the panel's button; pressed in a pane that is not the one in focus it moves the panel there (opening it if shut) rather than closing it. A page the panel opens (journal, lore, a budget) replaces that pane's conversation alone.
- **Verified for the blueprint:** one thread's conversation mounts in a pane without the quest-wide `Conversation`: `ThreadPane` already took one thread's `MissionDetail`; it moved to `conversation/threadPane.tsx`, and each pane reads its own thread (`conversation/paneThread.tsx`), so panes beside each other stay current.
- **Split:** `conversation.tsx` (387 lines) is now 176: `ThreadPane`, the pages (`questPages.tsx`) and the panel (`questPanelOf.tsx`) moved out; `agoOf` moved to `foundation/formatting.ts` (still exported from `questRecency.ts`). New pure `shell/paneChain.ts` (20 tests), `paneHost.tsx`, `paneStrip.tsx`, `paneResizer.tsx`. No contracts or service change.
- **Choices to redirect (the owner's draft items):** folded panes give their room to the others equally; the reply line shows title, state, waiting count and activity; the details panel follows the pane pressed last, and a freshly opened pane takes focus; closing is on every pane but the first (the quest's), folding on every pane but the deepest.
Reason: The owner's request, [conversation-panes](../capabilities/conversation-panes/specifications.md), batch 2 of the [blueprint](../capabilities/conversation-panes/blueprint.md).
Validation: Biome clean; `bun run typecheck` passes on all workspaces; `apps/app` tests: 83 files, 633 passed, 6 skipped. The full suite: 307 files passed and 7 failed (29 tests, all in `packages/service` worktree, own-copy and folder-picker tests that stop at `git init` copying hook templates into a folder the thread's sandbox cannot write; none touch this change). Looked at in a throwaway Electron window, see [the verification note](../verification/2026-11-19-conversation-panes.md): a quest with errands to three levels opened, folded, unfolded, closed, reopened and resized; the sidebar opened a four-pane chain; the details panel followed the pane pressed; a 1100 px window showed two panes and two strips; light and dark; Docked. Not seen: a phone-width quest (unchanged code path), a quest with long real conversations, the black and white theme, a real harness.

## CHG-0557 · 2026-11-19 · Conversation panes, batch 1: the sidebar floats over the panes

Type: Code
Event: Delivery
Scope: `apps/app` (shell, settings, foundation), conversation panes batch 1

Summary: The sidebar is an overlay by default. The owner's go-ahead to build was given on 2026-11-19 (CHG-0556's question).
- **Overlay:** the sidebar floats over the panes and the details panel without moving them. The pointer opens it at the window's edge after 220 ms (a pointer that comes back before the delay cancels it); it closes 160 ms after the pointer goes past the panel or out of the window, on a press outside it, on Escape, and when something is chosen in it. It stays open while a quest or a realm is dragged (a drag at the edge also opens it), and while its own edge is dragged to resize it. Only a mouse pointer opens it; touch has the button.
- **By hand:** ⌘B or Ctrl+B and a new button in the top bar (beside the host menu on the left, after Chronicler's mark on the right) show and hide it. A panel opened by hand stays until the pointer has visited it and left, or a press lands outside it.
- **Mode and side:** two choices in the Settings Look card, Sidebar (Overlay or Docked) and Sidebar edge (Left or Right, shown for the overlay), applied at once with no reload. Overlay on the left is the default. Docked keeps today's frame, the round handle and ⌘B included; docked is always on the left (the edge applies to the overlay only).
- The panel is slid out of the window, not unmounted, so the sidebar keeps its folds and a dialog it opened (a new realm, a move) is not closed by the pointer leaving.
- `shell/shell.tsx` (453 lines) is split before growing: the details panel (Quest and Companions tabs) is now `useDetailsColumn.tsx`, its styles `shellStyles.ts`; the overlay is `sidebarMode.ts` (rules), `useSidebarOverlay.ts` (pointer, keys, drag) and `sidebarOverlay.tsx` (the panel). The mode and side live in `foundation/sidebarPreference*.ts`, not `railStorage.ts` as the blueprint says, because the Workshop sets them and `settings/` may not import `shell/` (`shell/` already imports `settings/`).
Reason: The owner's request for a Slack-like window with an Arc-style sidebar, [conversation-panes](../capabilities/conversation-panes/specifications.md), batch 1 of the [blueprint](../capabilities/conversation-panes/blueprint.md).
Validation: Biome clean; `bun run typecheck` passes on all workspaces; the new rule tests (22) pass; looked at in a throwaway Electron window on the fake harness, see [the verification note](../verification/2026-11-19-conversation-panes.md): hover opens after the delay and not before, leaving, a press outside, Escape and choosing close it, ⌘B and the top bar button open it, the right edge and Docked work, and the Look card changes both with no reload. Not seen: a real drag of a quest while the panel is open, a touch device, the dark and black-and-white looks of the sidebar, a mouse wheel at the edge.

## CHG-0554 · 2026-11-19 · A connector's key is entered in its own panel, not in the Vault tab

Type: Code
Event: Agreement and Delivery
Scope: `apps/app` (settings, connectors), `packages/service` (connector card wording)

Summary: Asked whether the Composio key is set in the Composio tab, I said it was entered in the Vault tab, with only a button leading there. The owner answered that it has to be in the connector's tab, and that the Vault is for other secrets, such as passwords for browsing online.
- The Connection block of a connector's panel holds a field for each kind of secret the connector needs (Composio: API key): add, replace or remove it, with its stored state. The field is the Vault's own (`SecretField`, moved out of `vaultCard.tsx`); the value goes to the service once and is never read back.
- When the Vault refuses to store (its read-denial rule, a locked or lost key), the panel shows the Vault's reason and a button to the Vault tab, where Claude Code's protection is verified. The rule is unchanged.
- The Vault tab keeps its state, protection and verification, and lists only the secrets no panel holds: those of a connector that is not installed or is being removed, so they can still be removed. `leftBehindOf` decides it.
- Composio's card says "Add your API key below." instead of "in the Vault."
- Not done: a store for secrets that belong to no connector (passwords for browsing online). The service's `PutSecret` takes a connector id and a kind, and the specification has no such secret yet; it needs its own agreement before it is built.
Reason: The owner's request.
References: [specification](../capabilities/connectors/specifications.md#the-vault), ADR 0022, CHG-0551.
Validation: Biome clean; `bun run typecheck` passes on all workspaces; the connector, contract and app tests pass (103 files, 789 passed, 7 skipped), with a new test of `leftBehindOf`. Not seen in a window, and no key was stored.
