## CHG-0791 · 2026-11-27 · One shared chain editor for a preset, with Save and Discard instead of autosave

Type: Code
Event: Delivery
Scope: `apps/app/src/editors/presetChain.ts`, `apps/app/src/editors/presetChainEditor.tsx`, `apps/app/src/editors/useDraftStack.ts`, `apps/app/src/editors/presetEditor.tsx`, `apps/app/src/settings/workshopCompanions.tsx`, `documentation/capabilities/companion-presets/specifications.md`
Summary: A preset's Companion and backups are edited in one list (`PresetChainEditor`), the same in the thread tab's edit mode and in the Workshop's Default and preset panels. Row 1 is the Primary, the others are Order 2 to 6 (the limit stays six, `maxChain`, so the contracts, Rituals and paired devices are unchanged). Rows reorder by drag or by the up and down buttons, and each reads model, account (only when the harness has two or more accounts) and effort. Edits are a draft held in `useDraftStack`: nothing is saved until Overwrite (with its confirmation), and Discard restores the saved chain, where the Workshop and the edit mode saved every change at once. The Workshop keeps Default, the tier hints, and add, rename and delete of a preset. The chain rules are pure functions in `presetChain.ts`.
Reason: The owner asked (2026-11-27) for one editor everywhere with Save and Discard semantics, keeping every Workshop control and the six-model limit.
References: [companion presets specifications](../capabilities/companion-presets/specifications.md), CHG-0789.
Validation: Biome, the workspace typecheck, and `apps/app` tests (134 files, 1,098 passed, 6 skipped) pass; the new `presetChain` and `useDraftStack` tests are new files. In real Chrome (react-native-web harness, not committed) a drag reorder, Discard, and Overwrite with confirmation were observed.
Not run: the Expo app against a live service, a desktop window or phone (native drag has no grip, the arrow buttons remain). Not built: the sidebar preview of an unsaved draft, "Save as new" in the editor, the Workshop's new-preset draft on the shared editor, removal of the Workshop panel.
