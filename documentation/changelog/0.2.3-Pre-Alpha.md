## CHG-0661 · 2026-11-23 · Manage Companion presets

Type: Code
Event: Delivery
Scope: Workshop presets and the Companions tab

The Workshop can add custom presets, edit their Companions and backups, and rename or delete any saved preset.

## CHG-0664 · 2026-11-23 · Force the host update over running turns

## CHG-0668 · 2026-11-23 · Add the Islands theme

Type: Code
Event: Agreement and delivery
Scope: Look, window frame

Summary: The Workshop's Look card offers a fourth theme, **Islands**, from the islands prototype (`documentation/art-direction/islands-prototype.html`). In it the wide window's sidebar (docked, pinned, or overlay), content panel, and quest panel float as islands with a hairline and a shadow on a canvas under a top bar that lies on the canvas. Its light and dark palettes are the prototype's. While Islands is the theme, the Look card adds **Canvas** (cool glow, warm glow, flat), **Gap** (4–20), **Radius** (6–22), and **Lift** (none to deep), kept in the browser's storage and applied with no reload. The narrow window keeps its flat frame.
Reason: The owner chose the islands prototype, asked to keep its gap, radius, and lift controls and its theme, and kept only the hairline style (2026-11-23).
Validation: Focused tests (Islands settings, theme list, palette contrast, desktop icon) pass, and the app and desktop typechecks pass. A throwaway desktop instance showed the theme in light and dark, the pinned sidebar, a quest, and the Look card's controls changing the frame live.
Evidence: [Islands theme verification](../verification/2026-11-23-islands-theme.md).
