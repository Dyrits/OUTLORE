8 · Growing floating bar

Only Chronicle and Realms are on the bar at first. Choosing a realm makes Quests grow in; choosing a quest makes Errands grow in. Tap a tab to show its choice, or hold it for 0.5 s to change it. Choosing a different realm takes Errands away again.

The top right carries the desktop pane header's actions for the level shown: New quest on a realm; New errand, Mark as done, Archive and the Quest panel on a quest; the same without the panel on an errand. The top-left button opens the side menu, which now lists the hosts.
