9 · Chat landing, composer above the bar

Draft 8's growing bar, with one change: choosing a realm, a quest or an errand lands in its chat. The pane's own tabs (Missives, Quests, Errands, Companions…) sit under the header, as the app already draws them on a phone, so nothing is lost. The composer floats above the bar, as in Claude Duet.

Tap a tab to show its chat; hold it for 0.5 s to choose another. The composer goes while the Chronicle shows. Try the Quests tab in a realm's pane: it lists the quests and opens one in its chat.
