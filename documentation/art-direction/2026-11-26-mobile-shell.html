Ten directions for the phone shell

The owner wants the phone to feel like Claude Duet or the Slack mobile app, with a bottom bar like theirs (2026-11-26). Today a narrow window has a flat top bar whose menu button carries the Chronicle's unread count and opens a full-screen list of places: the Chronicle, then realms and quests, then Lore, Archive, Reserves and Settings (NarrowTopBar in topBar.tsx, menuPlaces in placeCatalog.ts). Each phone below is live: press the bar to change screens, press a quest to open its conversation, press Back to return, and answer an Attention card to watch the badge count go down. The ten differ in how the app is organised (what the bar holds, where the realms and the places live, where the Attention count shows), not in icon styling. The data is made up from Outlore's own work; nothing here changes the app.

Duet's ideas used here: attention first, one ongoing thread with the assistant, a morning brief, approvals shown before action, and seeing and steering what runs (research note). Slack's: bottom tabs Home, DMs, Activity and You, a channel list with unread badges, and a composer pinned above the bar. The glossary says to write Attention, not "needs you", so the badges and sections use Attention.

Recommended: D first, then B

D Chronicle / Realms / Quests / More

It is the smallest change that gives the Slack-like bar: the four tabs are the sections the menu already has, so every current screen keeps its place and Back keeps its meaning.

The Attention badge sits on the first tab, as the menu button carries it today, and the footer places (Lore, Archive, Reserves, Settings) stay one sheet away, taken straight from menuPlaces.

The cost is low, and the bar hides in a conversation so the composer has the bottom to itself.

B Chronicle / Ask Chronicler / Quests

It is the closest to Duet: attention first on the first tab, with the morning brief and the approvals, and one ongoing thread with the Chronicler as the centre of the bar, so the assistant is always one press away.

It also keeps the bar visible in a quest, so the owner can hop to the Chronicle to answer an approval without a Back press.

It costs a little more than D (a new Chronicler tab and a places screen behind the avatar), and it could be built on D: D's bar with Chronicler in a centre slot.
