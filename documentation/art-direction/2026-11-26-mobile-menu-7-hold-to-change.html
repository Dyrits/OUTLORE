7 · Floating tab bar, hold to change

Four floating tabs: Chronicle (logo only), Realms, Quests and Errands. Tap a tab to show what is chosen; if nothing is chosen yet, it opens a floating sheet to choose one. Hold a tab for 0.5 s to open the sheet and change the choice. Quests show two swords until a quest is chosen, then one sword. Choosing a realm clears its quest and errand; choosing a quest clears its errand.
