Plan usage: three display alternatives

The owner likes the popup and dislikes the circles (2026-11-24). Antigravity makes the circles harder still: four limits (a 5-hour and a weekly limit for each of two model groups) would be four concentric rings around one logo. Each alternative below shows the rail strip (what is always visible) and the popup (what hovering or tapping opens) for the same data: Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode Go (three windows), and Antigravity (four limits, the Claude and GPT group untouched). Figures are the real Antigravity reading of 2026-11-25: Gemini models 62% of the weekly limit left, 96% of the 5-hour limit left; the Claude and GPT group has never been used, so the CLI reports no reset time and the popup says "not started".

A · Worst limit as a number

Each provider is its logo with one chip: the percent left of its tightest limit. The chip turns orange from 80% used and red at 100%. The popup groups by model group, with a bar per limit.
