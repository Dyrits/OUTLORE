## What it is

- A standalone iOS and Android app (and claude.ai on a computer) where "Claude works as a proactive agent"; a research preview opened by waitlist on 2026-10-21, US Pro, Max, and Ultra plans (announcement; product page FAQ "Who can use Claude Duet and what does it cost?").
- "It comes to you with what needs your attention, completes tasks you ask of it, keeps working even when you step away, and checks with you before it acts on your behalf" (announcement, first paragraph).
- Powered by Claude Opus 5.5; its judgment is the product: "decide what needs you, what can wait, and when to check with you first" (announcement, "Put your attention where it matters most").

## The attention-first phone loop

- "Each morning, it sends you a short brief on the most important items across your connected apps. Throughout the day, it keeps tabs on those apps and messages you only when something needs your attention" (announcement).
- "Claude Duet comes to you. It tells you what needs your attention each morning, takes tasks you hand it by message, and reaches out when one is done or needs your approval" (product page, "How is Claude Duet different from Claude?").
- "You can tell it what you want to hear about and how often" (announcement, "Receive a message when something needs you"); it checks connected accounts "every few hours, because checking nonstop would use up your plan's limits much faster".
- One ongoing thread carries the owner's Claude memory, skills, and connectors; "a quick reply and a weeklong project both start in the same place", reached "through typing or voice" (announcement, "Your context from Claude is the foundation"; product page FAQ).

## Work, approval, and steering

- It works on "a virtual machine with its own browser, so it doesn't require your laptop or phone to be on"; "You'll receive a notification when the task is complete or needs your review" (announcement, "Work continues when you step away").
- "Before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document, or deletes anything, it shows you exactly what it's about to do and waits for your approval. A few things are off-limits even with your approval, like trading securities or moving money between accounts" (announcement, "Important decisions stay with you").
- "You can see what Claude Duet has done and is doing for you, and steer it" (product page, "Built for judgment").
- Its own limits: "it can't yet show you its browser"; it may "misread a thread or miscalculate what's urgent" (announcement, "Join the research preview"; product page "What isn't ready yet?").
- It is separate from the main Claude app "for now… What we learn from early users will shape how the two work together" (announcement, last section).

## What Outlore took (2026-11-22, the phone Chronicle batch, CHG-0647)

- The phone menu opens the Chronicle with its unread badge, and the narrow top bar's menu button carries the same count from every phone screen: what needs the owner shows without opening a list.
- Back from a quest or errand opened from the Chronicle returns to the Chronicle: the brief → conversation → answer → brief loop.
- Already Outlore's, matching Duet: the morning brief is the daily background check-in posted to realm Missives (CHG-0633); "waits for your approval" is action approval, answerable from the phone in the conversation; "see what it is doing and steer it" is the conversation's steps with Stop and Steer.
