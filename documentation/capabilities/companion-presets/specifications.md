# Companion presets

Status: **Preset management and Save as a preset are implemented in CHG-0661; the Companions tab's split layout, Custom entry and edit mode are built in CHG-0789 and await the owner's look in a running app.** The owner still needs to review the Workshop and Companions tab in a running app.

## Problem and outcome

Choosing a Companion means picking a harness, an account, a model, an effort, and often a list of backup Companions, again for each quest, errand, and Ritual. A **Companion preset** is a name for such a choice, so the owner picks "Heavy" or "Cheap digest" instead of redoing the whole choice. The Workshop's tiers (Light, Balanced, Heavy) are already presets in all but name; this makes them one kind of thing with the owner's own.

## Agreed behavior

- **What a preset is** (CHG-0242): a name, one Companion (harness, provider account, model, effort), and optionally an ordered list of backup Companions (at most five, as everywhere, [thread-panel](../thread-panel/specifications.md#backup-companions)). Preset names are unique.
- **Tiers are presets**: the Workshop's Light, Balanced, and Heavy tiers become presets of one list with those names. Outlore still picks a Companion by the request's complexity among Light, Balanced, and Heavy only (an unset one is not offered), and never picks a preset the owner made otherwise ([mission-coordination](../mission-coordination/specifications.md)). The owner can add custom presets and rename or delete any saved preset. Renaming a tier changes its display name and keeps its routing role.
- **Where a preset is chosen** (CHG-0242; CHG-0258 answered for the first build: the three tiers, in the Companions tab for a quest, an errand and its draft, and Chronicler, and in each backup fold): with one chooser, everywhere a Companion is chosen: the Companions tab for a quest, an errand, and Chronicler (right-hand column); the forms that start a quest or an errand; a Ritual's Companion and each of its fallbacks; and the card Chronicler proposes a quest with, in place of "Customize the Companion". What the build covers so far is under Not built, and excluded.
- **Choosing copies the whole stack** (owner correction, 2026-11-13): a preset is its primary Companion and its ordered backup list together. Choosing it replaces both, in one operation (whether an empty preset list clears the thread's backups is provisional, below). Editing or deleting the preset later leaves every quest, errand, and Ritual that already used it as it is.
- **Choosing on an existing thread** is a handover with the same rules as choosing its Companion by hand: between turns, refused while the thread runs or waits for an answer, and the line in the conversation when a turn starts on the new Companion ([thread-handover](../thread-handover/specifications.md)). A refusal leaves the primary and backups unchanged. Whether a preset with no backups clears the thread's list or leaves it as it is is provisional (see below).
- **Where presets are made**: a **Presets** card in the Workshop (add, rename, change a preset's Companion and backups, delete), and **Save as a preset** in the Companions tab of a quest, an errand, an errand draft, or a realm conversation. The new preset copies the primary Companion and its ordered backups. Changes stay in a draft until the owner chooses **Overwrite** or **Save as new**, and **Discard** restores the saved chain.
- **The Companions tab shows presets as a list** (owner's request, 2026-11-27, settled with "both"): one row for each preset, with its name and a summary (model, effort, account, number of backups), and, when the thread's whole stack matches no preset, a **Custom** entry. When the column is at least `splitLeastWidth` (480) wide, measured on the panel itself and not the window, the list is a left navigation and the thread's Companion details (the Companion fold, the backup folds, Overwrite, Save as a preset) sit to its right; narrower, the list is the Preset card above the details, as before, with Custom as its last row. The panel is resizable, so the layout follows its width as it changes.
- **Custom is the unsaved Companion** (owner's request, 2026-11-27): changing the Companion or its backups after choosing a preset leaves the preset untouched and moves the selection to the **Custom** entry, subtitled "not saved yet", which holds the thread's stack; the preset it came from keeps its row, unchecked. Pressing Custom does nothing, since it is the thread's state. **Save as a preset** and **Overwrite** work from it; once saved, a preset matches the stack and the Custom entry disappears. It is not shown while the thread's backups are unread, or when there are no presets (the tab has no list then).
- **Edit mode is how a preset itself is changed from the tab** (owner's request, 2026-11-27): while a preset is selected (the thread's stack matches it), an **Edit** button at the top right of the details enters edit mode. The title row then reads "Editing <name>", the same spot holds **Done**, and the details are replaced by the preset's own editors: its Companion fold and its backup folds, the same pickers as the thread's. The editor is one shared **chain editor** (the same in the thread tab and the Workshop's Default and preset panels): one ordered list of at most six Companions (row 1 is the Primary, the others are Order 2 to 6), reordered by drag or the up and down buttons. Changes are a **draft**: nothing reaches the preset until the owner chooses **Overwrite** (after its confirmation), and **Discard** restores the saved chain; a failure shows on an error line. Each row reads model, account (only with two or more accounts) and effort. Effort is chosen on one segmented bar. **Save as new** keeps the draft as a new preset under a name. While a preset is edited, its row in the list (the left navigation, and the Workshop's custom list) shows the draft marked **unsaved**, and in the left navigation each preset lists its models one under another. A draft is kept by preset when the owner presses Done, chooses another preset or switches a Workshop tab, and resumes on return while the saved chain is still the one it began from; the row says unsaved only while a draft that differs from the saved chain exists. A preset not made yet (the Workshop's Add preset) is edited in the same editor and can only be saved under a name or discarded. **Edit mode changes the preset's definition only and never this thread's Companion**, which is the invariant of choosing copying the stack: a thread that was on the edited preset moves to the Custom entry holding its old stack when no preset matches that stack (if another preset still holds the same stack, that preset is selected instead), and it rejoins the edited preset by pressing that preset's row, an ordinary handover with the usual refusals. Pressing a row while editing leaves edit mode and chooses that preset.
- **Workshop display and editing**: built-in and custom presets show Harness, Model, and Backups in the same table, and their chevrons open the selected preset in the Companions panel. The panel is the same list and details as a thread's Companions tab: the list holds Default, the three tiers and the custom presets, each with its models one under another (a tier not set says so), the one shown marked and any draft marked unsaved, then **+ Add preset**; the details hold the tier's hint, the one shared chain editor, and **Rename** and **Delete** (Default has the editor only). Wide enough, the list stands at the left of the details; narrower, above them. Add preset opens a draft that starts as a copy of the Companion the panel shows, edited in the same editor and saved under a name with **Save as new**; Discard drops it. **Save as new** from a saved preset creates the preset, selects it, and puts the source preset back to its saved chain. A thread's edit mode offers the same Rename and Delete under its editor; Default belongs to the Workshop alone, as the Companion no preset applies to.
- **A preset that cannot be used** (it names a removed or signed-out provider account, or a model its harness no longer lists) is shown as unavailable with the reason and cannot be chosen, as a backup that cannot be taken is passed over. It stays in the list so the owner can repair or delete it.
- **Hosts**: presets live on the host, with its provider accounts, and are shared by the devices paired with it ([device-pairing](../device-pairing/specifications.md)); a preset names accounts by their Outlore identity, never by a credential.

## Edge cases

- Preset names are trimmed and compared without regard to case. A duplicate asks for another name. The names Default, Light, Balanced, and Heavy are reserved (Light, Balanced, and Heavy for their respective tiers, and Default for the Workshop's default Companion).
- Deleting a preset that is a tier clears that tier; Outlore then falls back to the default Companion as it does for a tier not set.
- A preset whose primary Companion equals the thread's current one still replaces its backups, without a handover line. A preset is marked selected only when its primary and ordered backups match.
- A preset's backup list is checked as a thread's is (at most five, none equal to its own Companion is not required).
- A preset edited in edit mode that another device renames or deletes meanwhile ends the edit mode: the details show the thread's own Companion again.
- Two presets with the same whole stack are both equal to the thread's stack; the first in the list is the one marked, as before, and Custom does not appear.
- A thread whose stack matches no preset because the owner edited its preset in the Workshop, or on another device, also shows the Custom entry; nothing in the thread changed.

## Acceptance

Checks an agent can perform:

- The Workshop's three tiers appear as presets named Light, Balanced, and Heavy, and Outlore's proposals still use them as before.
- A preset made in the Workshop is offered in the Companions tab, the new-quest and errand forms, a Ritual's pickers, and Chronicler's card; choosing it fills harness, account, model, effort, and backups.
- Editing a preset after a quest used it leaves the quest's Companion and backups unchanged.
- Choosing a preset on an idle quest hands it over and leaves the line in the conversation; on a running one it is refused.
- Provisional: choosing a preset with no backups clears the old list. The same primary with different backups still updates the list and is not marked as the same preset beforehand.
- Provisional: a new errand copies the chosen preset's backups instead of inheriting its parent's; the parent stays unchanged.
- **Save as a preset** makes a preset equal to the Companion in use and its backups; a name in use is refused.
- **Overwrite** appears beside Save as a preset in the Companions tab of a quest, an errand, or a realm conversation once its Companion or backups differ from the preset last chosen in that tab. After one confirmation it replaces that preset's whole stack (built-in tiers included); no other quest changes. In the Workshop, changes to Default or any saved preset also stay in a draft until **Overwrite** (after one confirmation) or **Save as new**, and **Discard** restores the saved chain.
- Changing a thread's Companion or one of its backups after choosing a preset shows the **Custom** entry, marked and subtitled "not saved yet", and unchecks the preset; choosing the preset again removes Custom. **Save as a preset** from it adds a preset, which is then marked, and Custom disappears.
- No Custom entry appears while the thread's backups are unread, or for a thread whose stack matches a preset.
- With the Companions column 480 wide or more, the presets are a left list and the details sit to its right; narrower, they are stacked with Custom as the card's last row; resizing the column switches between them.
- **Edit** on a selected preset opens edit mode ("Editing <name>", **Done**) with the preset's Companion and backup folds; changes stay in a draft until **Overwrite** sends the whole stack (`SetStack`) or **Save as new** creates a preset, and **Discard** restores the saved chain, without changing the thread's Companion; after an Overwrite, a thread that was on the preset shows Custom selected with its old stack when no preset matches that stack (if another preset still matches that stack, that preset stays selected), and pressing the overwritten preset's row hands the thread over to the edited stack.
- A preset naming a signed-out account is listed as unavailable with its reason and cannot be chosen.
- On a paired device, the host's presets are the ones shown.

Human acceptance: the owner reviews the look of the Presets card and of the chooser in the Companions tab, and that choosing "Heavy" in a quest feels as quick as the by-hand choice it replaces. For CHG-0789 the owner also looks, in the desktop window and on the phone, at the split at 480 and wider (and whether 480 is the right width), the word **Custom**, the **Edit** and **Done** buttons at the top right, and that a thread on an edited preset moving to Custom reads as intended.

## Skills bound to a preset (proposed, not built)

Proposed by the owner on 2026-11-16 as an idea ("attach each skill to a preset of companions, so that when invoked it uses that preset"). The owner confirmed the semantics the same day: invoking runs the skill in an errand (below). The rest is the Companion's reading of it and awaits the owner's review; nothing is built.

- **A skill may name one preset.** The link is kept by Outlore on the host, beside the presets, not in the skill's file, so it holds for every harness and leaves the skill's own format alone. A skill with no link behaves as today.
- **Invoking runs the skill in an errand** (owner, 2026-11-16): it starts with the preset's whole stack (primary and ordered backups); the invoking thread keeps its own Companion and receives the result. No Companion changes inside a conversation. Rejected: a handover of the invoking thread for one turn, and a mere suggestion.
- **Presets only at first** (owner, 2026-11-16): Light, Balanced, and Heavy, until the owner's own named presets are built (see Not built).
- **A preset that cannot be used** (unavailable, as above) does not block the skill: the skill runs on the invoking thread's Companion and the conversation says why.
- **Choosing and clearing the link** is in the Workshop, per skill, with the same chooser as elsewhere; deleting a preset clears the links to it.
- **Acceptance (once agreed)**: a skill linked to Heavy, invoked from a Light quest, runs on Heavy's stack and its result returns to the quest; an unlinked skill runs as before; a link to an unavailable preset falls back with a line in the conversation; deleting the preset clears the link.

## Requirements covered

- Provider credentials are never stored, collected, or relayed ([requirements](../../requirements.md#privacy-and-data)): a preset names an account by its identity only.
- Bring your own provider, and the cost of running Outlore, are unchanged: a preset only names choices the owner could make by hand.
- Paired-device traffic stays end-to-end encrypted ([device-pairing](../device-pairing/specifications.md)): presets reach a device over the same channel as other host data.

## Provisional choices

The owner's correction (2026-11-13, "selecting a preset should do a full stack of companions") settles that choosing a preset puts a Companion and its backups in place together. The implementation decided the following alone, so each awaits the owner's confirmation; none is attributed to the owner:

- **A preset with no backups clears the thread's list.** CHG-0242 said it leaves the list as it is.
- **A backup fold offers no presets.** Choosing a Companion for one backup is by hand, because a preset there would have to discard the rest of its stack; CHG-0258's answer put presets in each backup fold.
- **Outlore's proposal card carries the tier's whole stack**, and a quest opened from it starts with the tier's backups instead of the Workshop's default list. CHG-0258 said the card's chooser stayed unbuilt. This contradicts [thread-panel](../thread-panel/specifications.md#backup-companions), which says a quest's top thread starts with a copy of the Workshop's list.
- **A Ritual Outlore creates on a tier takes the tier's backups as fallbacks**, shown in the approval summary; naming an effort, harness, or model for it gives no fallbacks. [Rituals](../rituals/specifications.md) carries a pointer to this.
- **An errand opened with a chosen preset starts with its stack**, not a copy of its parent's list, as [thread-panel](../thread-panel/specifications.md#backup-companions) says it does.
- **The Workshop saves one tier at a time** (a change names only the tiers it changes); two quick edits of one tier's Companion and its backups can still overwrite each other until the settings are read again.
- **"Customize the Companion" on Chronicler's card keeps the chosen chip's backups**, and the card sends the list it shows: the service leaves the Workshop's default list unchecked when the card sends exactly that list.
- **A settings change naming a preset without `backups` is refused**, so an older paired device cannot wipe a preset's backups; stored presets saved before presets had backups still read with none.
- **A preset whose backup names a removed account** cannot be chosen, and a quest opened from a card without a stack keeps the Workshop's default list unchecked; removing an account does not scrub it from the Workshop's lists.

## Not built, and excluded

- Built (CHG-0260): Light, Balanced, Heavy each hold a primary and up to five ordered backups; the Workshop edits and counts those backups. Choosing a tier on an existing thread or a draft errand copies the complete stack. The Companions tab shows the presets in a card of their own above the thread's Companion, whose fold keeps the by-hand model choice (the owner's request, 2026-11-13, after seeing the presets inside the Companion fold). Outlore's quest card carries the tier's full stack, even when its primary equals Default; a Ritual created by Outlore on a tier takes its backups as fallbacks.
- CHG-0661 adds custom presets, rename and delete for built-in and custom presets, and Save as a preset. Custom presets are offered wherever the existing tier stack chooser was available, including Chronicler's quest card.
- The unavailable-preset explanations and the chooser in the owner's Ritual form are still pending.
- Sharing presets between hosts or exporting them; presets that change by themselves (a preset follows no model release); Outlore choosing presets of the owner's own; a preset that also sets the budget or the approval level.
