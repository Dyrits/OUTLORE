## Decisions

- The fill is the conversation's own (`colors.page`), not a white literal, so both looks behave: `#fafafa` in One light, `#f3ece3` in Iskios light, `#282c33` in One dark, `#172230` in Iskios dark. Recorded in CHG-0503.
- The creation rows keep their fill under the pointer, and only ordinary rows still fill on hover (CHG-0504). The border, icon and label alone say "button".
- An empty section's note uses the realm note's look and hides while folded, so folding never leaves a sentence about an unseen list (CHG-0505).
- "a new" went to the two sidebar labels and to the home screen button, so one label names the one action; the owner's "real" was read as "realm"; the doubled "to" in "No realm to visit to yet." and the lower-case "borderlands" were corrected (CHG-0505, CHG-0506). Whether the home screen should have changed is the open question below.
- Work after the `0.1.9-Prototype` tag goes in the next version's changelog file, here `0.1.10-Prototype.md`, created with CHG-0503.
