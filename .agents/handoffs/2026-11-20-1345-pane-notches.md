## Goal

Review the folded-pane notches in the desktop window and adjust their look.

## State

- CHG-0592 replaces the 44 px folded strips with notches: a folded pane takes no width and its notch sits, centered vertically, on the left edge of the next open pane, or the right edge of the last one.
- A notch shows only an icon (castle realm, compass Borderlands, sword quest, branch errand) with the state dot on quests and errands; the name is the accessibility label.
- Verified: the shell tests passed (141) with the layout expectations changed to zero-width folded panes, and Biome and the app typecheck were clean before the errand icon was aligned with the @ mention branch.
- Not verified: appearance in a window, overlap of a notch with pane content, and whether the narrow layout reads well.

## Decisions

- Notches take no room from the open pane and overlay its edge.
- Realm and Borderlands emblems were chosen by me; the owner has not confirmed them.

## Next
