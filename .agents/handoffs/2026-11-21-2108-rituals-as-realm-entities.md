## Decisions

- The Ritual keeps its underlying mission as the storage container (journal, budget, realm, conversation all reuse the mission machinery); code and stored identifiers keep their names — the observable model changed only. The service-journal guards (refusals at the message and tree seams) were the implementers' judgment, reviewed and accepted on the quest but not yet seen by the owner.
- Deleting a Ritual removes it with its runs and history permanently, in one confirmation; archiving a realm keeps each Ritual in place, switched off, marked "Realm archived".
- Where briefings were silent, the errands chose: rituals of an archived realm are invisible until the realm is restored; a realm tied to no folder cannot receive a directly created Ritual (pre-existing form limitation, recorded as lore); the composer is replaced by a muted line; the origin-link reads "Made from". All are listed under the Rituals section of the working state for the owner's call.
- This batch's records were renumbered after the rebase: CHG-0619 for the Delivery (CHG-0618 was taken by the thinking levels), `0073_ritual_origin_mission` for the migration. Commit messages of every session in this realm end with the provider-model signature line after the Co-Authored-By trailer.
