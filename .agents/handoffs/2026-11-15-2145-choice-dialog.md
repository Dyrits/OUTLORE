## Decisions

- Owner-approved (2026-11-15): multiple choices and an own answer ("Other") supported; Skip is the owner's act, recorded as `Skipped` and answered as a denial, distinct from `Withdrawn` (Stop, interrupt, restart); one dialog per call with up to four choices, no Always allow, no standing permission; the batch covers contract, service, Claude Code and the three interfaces, Codex and OpenCode adapters go to the backlog.
- A choice always waits for the owner, at every approval level and whatever the sandbox setting says (`answerPrompt` branches before any containment check).
- The contract carries `allowsOther` and `multiple` per choice so Codex (`isOther`, no multiple, `isSecret` unsupported) and OpenCode (`multiple`, `custom`) fit; the Claude-specific parts are `choicesOfPrompt` (reads the call) and `withAnswers` (writes the reply).
- Changelog numbering: CHG-0298 is taken by `worktree/android-apk` in the same new file `0.1.6-Prototype.md`; this work took CHG-0299, and the two entries must be merged into one file when the second branch lands.
