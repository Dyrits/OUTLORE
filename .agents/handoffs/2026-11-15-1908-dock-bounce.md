## Decisions

- `critical` (bounces until the user returns) rather than `informational` (one bounce). Chosen by the assistant, not yet confirmed by the owner; recorded in CHG-0292.
- No bounce while the window is focused: macOS shows nothing useful there and the user is already looking.
- The bounce does not depend on notification permission; it fires on every new open question, as the notification does. Only open questions trigger it, not other Attention reasons (budget, missing folder), because the existing hook watches only `ListOpenQuestions`.
