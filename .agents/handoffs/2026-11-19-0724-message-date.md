## Decisions

- The year is always shown, so a message from an earlier year is not ambiguous. Dropping it for this year's messages would be a small change in `dateOf`; the owner has not asked for that.
- The date is shown on agent replies only, because they are the only messages in the app that show a time.
- The date uses the device's locale (`toLocaleDateString([])`), as the time already does.
