## Goal

The owner can dictate a message instead of typing it, in every message box, on desktop, web and phone, then send it as usual. Speech is transcribed on the owner's own computer or phone with whisper.cpp; no vendor receives audio (ADR 0024, `documentation/architecture-decision-record/0024-speech-is-transcribed-on-the-owners-device-with-whisper-cpp.md` on the branch; the owner's rule is in the quest journal). Done looks like: the composer's microphone button works on desktop, then on the phone and in the web app, the transcript lands in the box for the owner to send, the agreed checks pass, and the owner accepts how it looks and feels.

## Decisions

- **No vendor receives audio; speech-to-text only; no direct voice for now.** Owner, 2026-11-18, in the quest's thread; the reason is [Local data](../../documentation/requirements.md#privacy-and-data). Recorded in ADR 0024 (accepted) and in the quest journal.
- **whisper.cpp on both apps, one engine and one model file.** Owner chose it to try first. ADR 0024 weighs the alternatives: Web Speech, the phone's own recognisers, vendor APIs, and other engines.
- **Model `base` (142 MiB), many languages.** Owner's answers, 2026-11-18. The first batch defaults to language `auto`.
- **The phone transcribes on the phone itself**, through a native module, not through the computer. Owner's answer, 2026-11-18. This makes the phone a second code path; the desktop uses the service's transcriber.
- **Batch 1 is the service's transcriber only** (CHG-0448 on the branch). Choices made within it, which the owner may redirect: refuse a recording that is not 16 kHz mono 16-bit WAV, rather than convert it; five-minute time limit; GPU on by default; settings from `OUTLORE_WHISPER_CLI`, `OUTLORE_WHISPER_MODEL`, `OUTLORE_WHISPER_LANGUAGE`, `OUTLORE_WHISPER_GPU`, read through `Config`.
- **Platform order**: the owner had no preference; desktop first is assumed, to confirm.
